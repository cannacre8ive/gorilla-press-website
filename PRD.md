# Product Requirements Document

## Purpose

The Gorilla Press website helps Oregon product brands understand the company's short-run label production offer and start a well-scoped quote request without relying on unsupported operating claims.

## Audience

- Oregon cannabis operators managing frequent product and compliance versions.
- Small product brands ordering launch quantities or short production runs.
- Designers and operators who need production file guidance.

## Core user stories

- As a buyer, I can understand the available label formats and finishing capabilities.
- As a cannabis operator, I can see how version-heavy production is handled.
- As a designer, I can find practical file-preparation requirements.
- As a prospect, I can submit a quote request once a production endpoint is configured.
- As a visitor, I can contact the business directly and understand that visits are by appointment.

## Requirements

1. All primary services are reachable from persistent navigation.
2. The experience remains usable at 320 px viewport width and with keyboard-only navigation.
3. Claims remain limited to confirmed business inputs; unknown specifications are not invented.
4. Failed or unconfigured form submissions never display a false success state.
5. Production pages and social assets are publicly accessible over HTTPS.

## Success metrics

- No broken internal navigation routes.
- Lighthouse accessibility and SEO scores of 90 or higher on the home page.
- Quote-start click tracking can be added when analytics consent and tooling are approved.
- A measurable quote-completion baseline can be established after the production endpoint launches.

## Out of scope for this release

- Payments, customer accounts, order tracking, and automated estimating.
- Unverified equipment, stock, turnaround, shipping, or compliance claims.
- A production quote backend or uploaded-artwork storage.
